========================================
PRIORITY QUEUE - QUICK REVISION
========================================

Priority queue gives the highest/lowest
priority element first.


1. MAX HEAP - DEFAULT

priority_queue<int> pq;

pq.push(10);
pq.push(30);
pq.push(20);

pq.top();     // 30


Largest comes first.


----------------------------------------

2. MIN HEAP

priority_queue<
    int,
    vector<int>,
    greater<int>
> pq;


Smallest comes first.


----------------------------------------

COMMON OPERATIONS

pq.push(x);

pq.top();

pq.pop();

pq.empty();

pq.size();


----------------------------------------

WITH PAIRS

priority_queue<
    pair<int,int>,
    vector<pair<int,int>>,
    greater<pair<int,int>>
> pq;


Useful for:

Dijkstra
Prim
Minimum-cost problems


MEMORY:

priority_queue<int>
    = MAX heap

greater<int>
    = MIN heap
